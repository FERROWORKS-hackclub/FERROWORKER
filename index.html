FERROWORKER

A one-metre-cube printer with a tool changer. We want it to be buildable in five days, even if you're 14. We're still working out the details.

What we're building

02

Tool Changer

The idea is one machine with three tools:

  • 3D Printer
  • Laser Cutter
  • CNC Drill

06

Got an old printer?

If you're done with a printer, we'd like to reuse its working parts in a FERROWORKER build. Broken or incomplete machines are welcome too.

  • What we accept
    FDM printers, extruders, hotends, stepper motors, rods, bearings, belts, and electronics. Broken or incomplete is fine.
  • What we cannot accept
    Resin vats, IPA-contaminated parts, or machines soaked in hazardous chemicals.
  • Shipping policy
    We'll pay postage in Australia. For international donations, the sender pays. We'll arrange a label or drop-off after we confirm the details.
  • Drop-off / mailing address
    We're in Brisbane, Queensland. We'll email the exact address once we've confirmed the donation.
  • Contact
    Email ferroworks@outlook.com.au with a photo or two and a short note about the printer.
